World Heart Day is observed on September 29 each year to unite people in the fight against cardiovascular disease (CVD), the leading cause of death around the globe. 1 Launched by the World Heart Federation , this day aims to raise awareness about heart disease and strokes and encourage individuals and communities to take proactive steps toward better heart health.
