Nurses are at the heart of healthcare — often the first to listen to a patient’s heartbeat, assess their breathing, and catch subtle signs that could change a care plan. Digital stethoscopes, especially those equipped with advanced features like ECG, sound visualization, and AI-powered analysis, are transforming how nurses deliver care. Explore five reasons every nurse should consider adding a digital stethoscope to their toolkit.
