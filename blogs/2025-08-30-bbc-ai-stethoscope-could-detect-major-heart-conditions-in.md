The BBC reports on a major study from Imperial College London and Imperial College Healthcare NHS Trust showing how Eko's AI-enabled stethoscopes are helping clinicians detect serious heart conditions earlier and more often — all during routine exams. With thousands of patients and hundreds of clinics involved, the research highlights how this technology could shift the standard of cardiac care in the UK. The story explores what this means for patients, providers, and the future of AI in medicine.
