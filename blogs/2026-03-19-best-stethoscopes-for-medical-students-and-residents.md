Medical students quickly learn that a reliable stethoscope becomes one of the most important tools during clinical training. Whether you're practicing physical exams or starting hospital rotations, the right device helps clinicians hear subtle heart and lung sounds that guide a treatment path. Early in training, distinguishing these sounds can be challenging, especially in busy hospital environments.
