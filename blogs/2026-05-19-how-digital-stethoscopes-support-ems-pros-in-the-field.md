For paramedics and EMTs, auscultation remains one of the fastest ways to get key patient information. Lung sounds, blood pressure readings, heart sounds, and subtle changes in breathing can all help guide urgent care decisions. But EMS environments are rarely quiet or controlled, and that can make it harder to hear what matters the first time.
